About NorthernForge

NorthernForge is an independent documentary channel about money: the booms, busts, schemes and bets that shaped how business works today.

How a video is made

  1. Research. Each story starts from public records: newspapers of the time, court and company records, government reports and books by historians.
  2. A fact sheet. Every figure, date, name and quote in the script comes from a fact sheet with its source. Nothing goes in a video that isn't on the sheet.
  3. Script and pictures. The script is written and checked against the fact sheet. Pictures are archive photographs, maps and public-domain film wherever they exist.
  4. Sources page. When a documentary goes public, its sources page goes up with it, so anyone can check a claim.

Use of AI

The narration is an AI voice, and the research, script and visuals are made with the help of AI tools. Every fact is checked against its source before a video is published.

Corrections

If we got something wrong, email northernforge.studio@gmail.com. Corrections are listed on the video's sources page.

Our tools

NorthernForge uses two private tools of its own: NorthernForge Pipeline, which uploads videos to the NorthernForge YouTube channel, and NorthernForge Publisher, which sends short clips to the NorthernForge TikTok account's inbox and posts to its X account. They are used only for NorthernForge's own accounts. See the privacy policy and terms of service.

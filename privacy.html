Privacy policy

NorthernForge Pipeline and NorthernForge Publisher · Last updated 26 October 2026

What this app is

NorthernForge Pipeline is a private tool used only by the owner of the NorthernForge YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own videos. It is not available to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Google data it uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to:

  • Upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's YouTube channel, and set their visibility and publish time.
  • Post comments on the channel's own videos.
  • Read the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, retention) to plan future videos.

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's private server. The app does not access any other Google data, does not collect data about viewers or other users, and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Limited use

NorthernForge Pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time from Google Account permissions, after which the stored token stops working.

TikTok and X

The same owner also uses NorthernForge Publisher to post the channel's own short clips to the channel's own TikTok and X accounts. From TikTok, it uses basic profile information (to confirm the right account is connected) and permission to upload videos to that account's inbox, where the owner reviews and publishes them. From X, it posts to the channel's own account and reads the like, reply, repost and view counts of those posts. It does not collect data about other users or viewers. It does not sell or share any data, and it does not use any data for advertising or for training AI models. Tokens are stored only on the owner's private server and can be revoked at any time in TikTok's or X's app settings.
